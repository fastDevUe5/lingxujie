# attenuation

Source: https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/attenuation

## attenuation A decrease in the volume and quality of a sound. In UE, Sound Attenuation can be set on any sound and includes options for Distance Attenuation, Air Absorption, Spatialization, Reverb, Occlusion, Focus, Priority, and Submix Sends.
